⚑ Flag Dash · Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

The short version: you can play Flag Dash without an account. If you sign in, we use your email only to sign you in and to save your game. Other players only ever see your runner name, flag and hero. We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't use advertising trackers.

Who we are

Flag Dash is an independent browser game. In this policy, "we" means the person who makes and runs Flag Dash. You can reach us at mr.severeh65@gmail.com.

Playing without an account

When you play as a guest, your progress (coins, purchases made with in-game coins, chosen runner, settings and screen layout) is saved only in your browser on your device. The game also creates a random device ID so that features like the leaderboard can tell devices apart. This ID is not linked to your name or email.

Clearing your browser data, or using a private window, erases this saved progress.

Services we use

Flag Dash relies on a few outside services. Each handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase: stores accounts, saved games, leaderboards and play counts, and carries online races and chat, and keeps friend lists and inbox messages.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google", to confirm who you are.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the game's website.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr and cdnjs: deliver the game's fonts and code libraries.

Like any website, these services may see your IP address and basic browser details when your device connects to them.

What we never do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We don't share your email with other players or with anyone else, except the services above that are needed to run the game, or if the law requires it.

Children

Flag Dash is a family-friendly game, and you don't need an account to play it. Children under 13 should only create an account with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you are a parent and believe your child created an account without your consent, email us and we will delete it.

How long we keep your data

  • Account and saved game: until you delete your account.
  • Friend list: until you remove a friend or delete your account.
  • Inbox messages: up to 60 days, or until you delete them or your account.
  • Leaderboard entries: kept with your runner name for the boards' history. Ask us and we will remove them.
  • Anonymous play counts: kept to track how the game is doing over time. They can't be traced back to you.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the game, tap the 👤 account button, then Delete my account. This removes your sign-in, your saved game, your friend list and your inbox.
  • See, correct or remove your data: email us at mr.severeh65@gmail.com and we will help within 30 days.
  • Play without an account: everything that doesn't need an account stays free for guests.

Security

Signing in uses one-time email links or Google, so there are no passwords for us to store. Each player's saved game can only be read or changed by that player's own account. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For important changes, we will also let players know in the game.

Contact
