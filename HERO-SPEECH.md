# Flag Dash: hero speech lines

These are the lines heroes say in speech bubbles during races. The game also reads them aloud (Settings → Voices).
Edit freely: change, add or remove lines, then send the file back and I will put it in the game.

Tips: keep lines short (under ~30 characters fits the bubble best) and family-friendly.

## Haitian Creole (runners carrying the Haiti flag)

The ID after each line is the name of its voice recording: record it as `voice/<id>.mp3` and it plays instead of the computer voice.

### When they hit a rival

- Pran sa!  (`ht-pran-sa`)
- Men li!  (`ht-men-li`)
- Ale lakay ou!  (`ht-ale-lakay-ou`)

### When they get hit

- M ap jwenn ou!  (`ht-m-ap-jwenn-ou`)
- Ou pral peye sa!  (`ht-ou-pral-peye-sa`)
- Tann mwen!  (`ht-tann-mwen`)
